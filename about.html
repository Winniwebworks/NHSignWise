About Sign Wise

A sign and graphics company in Henniker, New Hampshire, helping local businesses look sharp wherever they show up.

Signs, graphics and design, all in one place

Sign Wise LLC specializes in signs, vehicle graphics, vinyl decals and more. We also offer graphic design and logo design for businesses around New Hampshire, so you can go from an idea to a finished sign or a lettered truck with one team.

Based in Henniker, NH, we've completed dozens of projects for local businesses, from fuel companies and auto dealers to trucking companies, well drillers and builders. We're always happy to take on more.

  • Custom signs for storefronts, roadsides and buildings
  • Vehicle, truck and trailer graphics and lettering
  • Vinyl decals in any size or color
  • Logo design and graphic design for businesses
